Firmark

Founder and sole owner. Firmark is a web platform for structural/AEC work, built around one principle: maximum transparency, no black box. Every calc, assumption, and step is exposed and auditable — because a licensed PE has to put their seal on the output, and engineers are right not to trust tools they can't inspect.

  1. Browser CAD — a CAD environment rebuilt for the cloud, lightweight, built to be automatable.
  2. Calculation engine — tied directly into the CAD geometry.
  3. Automation engine — architectural plan in; calcs, bill of materials, and PE-stampable plans out in minutes, with human control gates at every stage from roof down to foundation.
  4. Project management layer.

Firmark never stamps plans — a licensed PE always seals the final output. It is a production tool, not a liability-bearing service.
